standards: [BSRIA BG29/2021, ASHRAE]
included:
  - Survey and water analysis of the running system
  - A cleaning plan agreed through the operator's change management
  - Side-stream filtration and controlled cleaning with no shutdown
  - Water quality monitoring throughout the work
  - Passivation and a treatment programme for the future
systems:
  - Operating CHW systems in colocation and enterprise data centers
  - Condenser water circuits
