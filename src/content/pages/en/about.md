Deotto Lab is a water treatment company based in Zagreb, Croatia. We advise on, clean and commission closed water systems, mainly the cooling systems of data centers. We have worked on projects in Croatia, France, Norway and Sweden.

## Our approach

**Independent.** We don't sell chemicals or cooling hardware, so our advice is vendor-neutral. We recommend what the system needs, not what we have in stock.

**Involved from the design stage.** Systems are easier to clean when they are designed for it. We review drawings and specifications early, so flushing connections, strainers and bypasses are in place before the pipework is built.

**Our own equipment.** We design and build our own filtration skids for closed-loop pre-treatment cleaning (CPC), and keep improving them on real projects.

**Working to recognised standards.** Pre-commission cleaning follows BSRIA BG29/2021, and water treatment follows ASHRAE guidance. Every stage is sampled, analysed and documented for handover.
