## Who we are

The controller of your personal data is Deotto Lab, Zagreb, Croatia. Our full company details are in the footer of every page. For any privacy question, write to us at the email address shown there.

## What we collect and why

We only collect the data you send us through the enquiry form or by email: your name, company, email address, phone number (optional), industry and your message.

We use it to reply to your enquiry and, if you want, to prepare an offer. The legal basis is your consent and steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR).

We don't use your data for marketing, and we don't sell it or share it with third parties, except for the service providers that host this website and deliver our email. They process data only on our behalf.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to handle them, and no longer than two years after our last contact, unless they lead to a contract. Contract records are kept for as long as the law requires.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability. You can withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting what was done before. You also have the right to complain to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP, azop.hr).

## Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. It stores one setting in your browser after you close the cookie notice, so the notice doesn't appear again. That setting never leaves your device.

The enquiry form is protected against spam. If that protection is enabled, it may use a strictly necessary security check from our hosting provider. It isn't used for tracking.
